100% Cloud Hosted: The WhatsApp browser runs securely in the background on Render. No browser window will pop up on your laptop. Everything is managed directly from this web page!
  1. 1. Open Cloud WhatsApp: Click "Launch Browser". The QR code will load in the box below.
  2. 2. Scan with Phone: In WhatsApp on your mobile phone, tap Settings > Linked Devices > Link a Device and scan the QR code.
  3. 3. Select Group: Once connected, choose your group from the "Select WhatsApp Group" dropdown below.
  4. 4. Extract & Export: Click "Start Extraction" and then "Export to Excel".
